2. Purpose and scope

The system runs a private housing scheme end to end. Every money movement posts to standard ERPNext documents (Sales Invoice, Payment Entry, Journal Entry, Purchase Invoice, Stock Entry, Asset), so the general ledger is the single source of truth and the real-estate screens add the plot, booking and society context around it.

In scopeOut of scope (this release)
Land and map (boundary, blocks, plots, infrastructure, KML / GeoJSON import) · CRM and WhatsApp · booking and payment plans · installments, receipts and recovery · transfers, cancellations, re-allotments, merges, surcharge waivers · agent and dealer commissions · society billing and collection · maintenance and complaints · development cost, procurement, stock and assets with depreciation · documents in English and Urdu · reports, dashboards and the owner portal. Online card payments for owners · construction (building) management after possession · live WhatsApp Business account (runs in mock mode until credentials are supplied) · Google Maps (supported, needs an API key).

7. Business rules and calculations

All money maths lives in one module (realestate/calc.py) with unit tests; values in brackets are the current settings in RE Settings.

Installment schedule

net = list price − discount fixed parts (booking, down payment, balloons, development, possession) = net × their % , rounded to Rs 1,000 each installment = (net − fixed parts) ÷ n , rounded to Rs 1,000 ; the last installment absorbs the difference Σ schedule = net (always exact)

Receipt allocation

a receipt pays unpaid rows oldest due date first ; it may not exceed the total outstanding

Late-payment surcharge

if days_late > grace (15): surcharge = max(minimum (Rs 500), overdue × rate (2% per month)) capped at overdue × cap (10%) one surcharge per booking per month

Cancellation

deduction = min(net × deduction % (10%), amount paid) refund = amount paid − deduction

Commission

commission = net × rate (agent 1%, dealer 2%) status: Accrued → Payable when paid ≥ net × 25% → Paid

Society bill

bill = Σ charge heads for the plot (fixed | rate × marla | rate for the plot size) payable = bill + unpaid earlier bills (arrears)
Rates, fees and percentages are settings, not code. Changing them affects new documents only; posted invoices keep the values they were created with.

12. Security and audit

  • Role-based access per §3; agents are scoped to their own records by server-side query conditions, not only by the screens.
  • Approval workflows for transfers, cancellations (refunds), re-allotments, merges and surcharge waivers; discounts above the limit need approval.
  • Submitted and cancelled financial documents cannot be deleted; corrections are cancel and amend.
  • Version history on every financial and settings document; the activity chain shows each record's history as linked blocks.
  • Secrets (API tokens, map keys) are stored encrypted in RE Settings; rates and fees are settings.
  • The owner portal reaches only the signed-in owner's records; staff preview is read-only.

14. Project and site setup

  1. Install. Bench with Frappe / ERPNext v16, Frappe CRM and WhatsApp; bench get-app realestate; create the site; bench --site <site> install-app realestate (setup runs roles, workflows, print formats and workspaces).
  2. Company and accounts. Company, fiscal year (July–June), cost centers per block, common and society; bank accounts; modes of payment (cash, bank transfer, cheque).
  3. RE Settings. Company and default project; discount limit; surcharge, transfer, re-allotment, cancellation and commission rules; billing day and due days; WhatsApp (mock or live) and fallback channels; map provider.
  4. Project and map. Create the RE Project and draw or import the boundary; land-use shares; blocks; plot sizes and prices; plots (import from KML / GeoJSON or create); infrastructure features.
  5. Sales setup. Payment plans; agents (users with Sales Agent) and dealers (Sales Partners); quick replies and WhatsApp templates.
  6. Society setup. Charge heads and size rates; maintenance plans; maintenance assignee.
  7. Development setup. Cost-head projects and budgets; site store; suppliers; asset categories with depreciation.
  8. Users. Staff users with roles; owner-portal invites from the customer page.
  9. Go live. Turn on the scheduler; run bench --site <site> re-acceptance until every check passes.
Demo commands (re-reset-demo, re-daily-data) only act when mock mode is on and the default project is flagged as the demo; they never touch real records.
