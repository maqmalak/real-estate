# Society Manager

- **Charge Heads**: fixed, per marla or by plot size. Bills are made on day 1 for every occupied plot, due in 15 days, with arrears carried.
- **Bulk Collection**: pick owners or a block and record many payments at once.
- **Complaints** from the portal or WhatsApp become tasks; assign a technician; the owner is told when it is done.
- **Maintenance Plans** create tasks ahead of their due date; **Infrastructure** shows each feature's build-out and upkeep.
- Reports: Society Bill Status, Defaulters, Dues by Charge Head, Income vs Expense.
