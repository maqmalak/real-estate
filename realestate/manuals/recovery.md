# Recovery Officer

- **Operations → Recovery & surcharges**: dunning notices, monthly surcharges and waiver requests.
- Log every call with its outcome and any **promise to pay** (amount and date); the follow-up appears on **Today**.
- Reminders go automatically 3 days before a due date and every 7 days when overdue — WhatsApp first, SMS if it fails.
- A surcharge waiver needs an Accounts Manager; it posts a credit note.
- Reports: Receivables Aging, Recovery Promised vs Received, Society Defaulters.
