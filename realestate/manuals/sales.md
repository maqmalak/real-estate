# Sales

## Your day
**Today** lists your site visits and follow-ups with call and WhatsApp buttons. Agents see only their own leads, customers and bookings.

## Leads and visits
1. **Leads & Deals**: record every enquiry with its source; an unknown WhatsApp number becomes a lead by itself.
2. Schedule a **site visit** — the invitation goes on WhatsApp — and record the result.
3. Move the deal: Opportunity → Site Visit → Negotiation → Booking. Booking creates the customer.

## Booking a plot
1. **Plots**: filter by block, size, price and status; open a plot and click **Book plot**.
2. The customer needs a CNIC and nominee. Pick the payment plan; a discount above 5% waits for the Sales Manager (**Actions → Approve discount**).
3. **Submit**: the schedule is built, the plot becomes Booked and the Sales Invoice is posted.
4. **Actions → Letters & forms**: allotment letter, schedule, application form, sale agreement — English or اردو.

## After the sale
Transfers, cancellations and re-allotments start from **Operations**; the Sales Manager approves them.
