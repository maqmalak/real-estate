# Land Manager

You buy the scheme's land before it is plotted: khasras and their co-owners, due diligence, the deal (iqrar nama), seller payments, plots instead of cash and the registry.

## Every day
- **Land Overview**: the land bank, overdue owners, hearings and registry deadlines. The bell reminds you 7, 3 and 1 days ahead.
- **Sellers & Payments**: what each owner is owed, what falls due this month, payments waiting for approval.

## A new khasra
1. **Khasras (records) → New**: mauza, khewat, khatooni, square, killa, khasra and the area in Kanal-Marla-Sarsai.
2. Add every **co-owner** with their share from the fard. Shares cannot exceed the khasra's area.
3. Move it along the **pipeline board** as the talks go: Identified → Under Negotiation → Due Diligence.

## Due diligence
Tick the nine checks as the papers come in (attach each one). The agreement stages stay locked until the seven required checks are **Verified**. Only an Admin can override, with a reason.

## The deal
1. **Land Deals → New**: date, description, land for, khewat / khatooni / square / killa.
2. One **owner line** per seller: owner, khasra, Kanals, Marlas, Sqft, unit (Acre / Kanal / Marla) and rate. Due = area in the unit × rate.
3. **Contract** tab: token, agreement amount, installments, possession and registry dates, clauses, witnesses, stamp paper. **Estimate transfer costs** fills stamp duty, registration, 236K and fees from the rate table.
4. **Build seller schedules**, then tick each seller's signature and both witnesses — the deal becomes **Executed**.
5. **Submit for approval**. When it is approved, purchase invoices are posted per owner.

## Paying sellers
- **Record seller payment** from the deal: cheque, pay order, transfer or cash, with the instrument and any withholding tax. Up to Rs 5,000,000 you can pay directly; above that it waits for an Admin or Accounts Manager.
- A token paid before approval is an advance.
- Ask the seller to sign the receipt; tick **Receipt signed by the seller**.

## Changes
- **Extension** (more time, extra amount), **amendments** (add a seller, change a share, cancel an owner) — each needs approval and posts its own entries.
- **Adjust with plots**: plots given instead of cash; approval books them to the owner.

## Registry
Mark each owner **Registry Done** / **Mutation Done**. When all are done the deal is **Completed** and the khasra **Acquired**. Accounts then capitalise the land cost.
