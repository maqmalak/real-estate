# Getting started

## Signing in
1. Open the address your office gave you and sign in with your email and password. Never share your account: every booking, receipt, payment and approval is recorded against the person who made it.
2. Forgot your password? Ask the Admin to reset it.

## Moving around
- The **sidebar** shows the groups of your role: Overview, Land Acquisition, Setup, Sales & Collections, Operations, CRM & WhatsApp, Society Operations, Development & Costs, Documents. Click a group to open it.
- **Search** (Ctrl + K) finds a plot, customer, booking, khasra or any record by number or name.
- The **bell** shows what is new: approvals waiting for you, overdue sellers, court hearings, expiring papers.
- **Back** returns you to the page you came from, with your filters.

## Light and dark
The moon / sun button in the header switches the theme. Printed documents and guides always use the light letterhead.

## Reports
**Overview → Reports** lists every report your role may open. Set the filters, then **Excel** or **PDF**.

## When something is refused
The ERP checks the scheme's rules and says why it refuses — for example "Khasra 1375 is oversold by 2K", "Agreement Signed needs the mandatory due diligence checks first" or "Clear the society dues before the transfer". Read the message: it tells you what to do.
