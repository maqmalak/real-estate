# Projects & development

- **Development & Costs → Overview**: every cost head (an ERPNext Project) with budget, actual, committed and payable, next to the works on the map.
- Buy through **Material Request → Purchase Order → Purchase Receipt** into the site store; issue to a cost head with a Stock Entry.
- Contractor running bills and NOC fees are purchase invoices on the cost head.
- **Assets** link to map features and depreciate by category.
- The **Land & Legal** head shows the land being bought and the capitalised land cost.
