# Accounts

## Receipts
**Receipts → New**: booking (or society bill / surcharge), amount, mode and reference. The amount is allocated oldest-due first and a Payment Entry is posted; the owner gets the receipt on WhatsApp.

## Land payments
- Deals are approved by an Admin or Accounts Manager — approval posts a **purchase invoice per owner** (Dr Land Under Acquisition, Cr Creditors - Land Owners).
- Seller payments above Rs 5,000,000 wait for your approval. Withholding tax goes to *Withholding Tax Payable - Land*.
- **Post transfer costs** on an approved deal posts stamp duty, registration, 236K and fees into the land cost.
- **Capitalise land cost** when a deal is completed and paid.
- Check **Land Owners Trial Balance**: owners' balances must equal the Creditors - Land Owners account.

## Approvals
Waivers, refunds, extensions, amendments and plot adjustments come to the bell and the urgent queue on the dashboard.

## Month end
Depreciation runs from ERPNext; the report centre has receivables aging, collection summary, society income vs expense and the land reports.
