Land acquisition — khasras, sellers, deals and payments

How land is bought before it is plotted: khasras and their co-owners per fard, due diligence, the deal sheet and agreement to sell (iqrar nama), approval and purchase invoices, seller payments, plots instead of cash, registry and the land cost.

Who: Land Manager (khasras, deals, payments), Admin / Accounts Manager (approvals), Accounts (payments, ledger).

1The land bank

  1. Land Acquisition → Land Overview shows Kanal acquired against the target, paid and payable to sellers and the average cost per Kanal.
  2. The map colours every khasra by stage; the acquired khasras are exactly the project boundary. Phase 2 khasras are still in the pipeline.
  3. Highlights call out overdue owners, disputes and their hearings, and land settled with plots.

2Khasras, co-owners and the pipeline

  1. Khasras (records) → New: mauza, khewat, khatooni, square, killa and khasra numbers (lists and ranges such as 43-44 or 5/2,16,17 are searchable), area in Kanal-Marla-Sarsai.
  2. Add each co-owner with their share from the fard; shares cannot exceed the khasra's area.
  3. On the pipeline board, drag a khasra to its next stage: Identified → Negotiation → Due Diligence → Token Paid → Agreement Signed → Payment in Progress → Registry / Mutation Done → Acquired.

3Due diligence and court cases

  1. Every khasra gets nine checks: fard-e-malkiat, jamabandi, mutation history, aks shajra, nishandehi, encumbrance, litigation, seller CNICs and legal opinion. Seven are required.
  2. The agreement stages stay locked until the required checks are Verified — or an Admin overrides with a reason.
  3. Due Diligence & Cases shows the matrix for all khasras, the court cases with the next hearing, and the land papers in the vault.
Hearings, payment due dates and registry deadlines come to the responsible person's bell 7, 3 and 1 days ahead.

4The deal (iqrar nama)

  1. Land Deals → New: deal date, description ("Falak Sher and Aalam Sher S/O Ghulam …"), land for, khewat / khatooni / square / killa.
  2. Add an owner line per seller: owner, khasra, Kanals, Marlas, Sqft, unit (Acre / Kanal / Marla) and rate. Due = area in the unit × rate (18K-10M at 25,000,000 per Acre = 57,812,500).
  3. The Contract tab: token, agreement amount, installments, possession and registry dates, clauses, witnesses, stamp paper and transfer costs. Build seller schedules splits the terms by each seller's share.
  4. The deal is Executed only when every seller and both witnesses have signed.

5Approval, purchase invoices and seller payments

  1. Submit for approval; an Admin or Accounts Manager approves. Approval posts a Purchase Invoice per owner: Dr Land Under Acquisition, Cr Creditors - Land Owners.
  2. Record seller payment: cheque, pay order, transfer or cash, with withholding tax. Up to Rs 5,000,000 it is paid directly; above, it waits for approval. A token paid before approval is an advance.
  3. Extensions (more time with an extra amount) and amendments (add a seller, change a share, cancel an owner) each go through approval and post their own entries.

6Plots instead of cash, registry and land cost

  1. Adjust with plots: pick available plots; on approval each is booked to the owner (as a customer) and netted against their land payable by a Journal Entry — no cash moves.
  2. Mark each owner Registry Done / Mutation Done; the deal becomes Completed and the khasra Acquired.
  3. Capitalise land cost (Admin / Accounts) moves the cost to the project's land cost; Module 2 counts it under Land & Legal.
