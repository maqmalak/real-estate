Setup — project, map, plots and rules

Everything the scheme needs before the first booking: the settings and rules, the project and its boundary, blocks, plot sizes, plots, payment plans, society charge heads and the users with their roles.

1Settings and rules

  1. Open Setup → Settings (RE Settings). Set the company, the default project and the discount limit.
  2. Rules are settings, not code: surcharge (grace days, % per month, minimum, cap), transfer and re-allotment fees, cancellation deduction and the agent / dealer commission.
  3. The Land tab holds sqft per Marla (272), the seller-payment approval limit (Rs 5,000,000), the province for transfer cost rates and the land accounts.
Changing a rate applies to new documents only; posted invoices keep the values they were made with.

2Project, boundary and land use

  1. Setup → Projects: the scheme with its total land and boundary — drawn on the map or imported (KML / GeoJSON) in Map Import.
  2. Land use (blocks, roads, public buildings, infrastructure, waste) must total 100% of the boundary; the acceptance checks warn if it does not.
  3. The boundary is the acquired land: once khasras are acquired, Merge into boundary (Land Overview, Admin) makes them the project map.

3Blocks, plot sizes, plots and payment plans

  1. Blocks carry their outline, plot size and planned plots. Plot sizes carry the base price and monthly maintenance.
  2. Plot records: number, block, size, street, corner / park-facing / boulevard flags, outline and price history.
  3. Payment plans: booking %, down payment, monthly or quarterly installments, balloons, development charges and possession share — the schedule always totals the net price.

4Users, roles and go-live

  1. Setup → Users & Roles: give each person one profile — Admin, Accounts, Land Manager, Sales Manager, Sales Agent, Recovery Officer, Society Manager, Projects, Maintenance or Customer.
  2. Agents see only their own leads, customers and bookings — enforced on the server, not just the screens.
  3. Acceptance checks (Admin) run every check from the specification: land use, layout, schedules, ledger, documents, roles and the land module.
