Collections & recovery — receipts, reminders and surcharges

How installments are collected: receipts allocated oldest-due first, reminders before and after the due date, the monthly late-payment surcharge, recovery calls and dunning, and waivers with approval.

1 · ReceiptCash, cheque, transfer
2 · AllocationOldest due first
3 · RemindersWhatsApp, SMS fallback
4 · SurchargeAfter 15 days grace
5 · RecoveryCalls, dunning, waiver
Who: Accounts, Recovery Officer, Accounts Manager (waivers).

1Receiving a payment

  1. Receipts → New: booking, amount, mode (cash, cheque, bank transfer) and reference.
  2. The amount is allocated to the oldest unpaid installments first and a Payment Entry posts against the booking's Sales Invoice; a receipt with QR goes to the owner on WhatsApp.

2Reminders, surcharges and recovery

  1. Reminders go 3 days before a due date and overdue notices every 7 days — WhatsApp first, SMS if it fails.
  2. After 15 days' grace a surcharge is raised monthly (2% of the overdue, minimum Rs 500, capped at 10%) as its own invoice.
  3. Operations → Recovery & surcharges: dunning notices, surcharges and waivers; a waiver needs Accounts Manager approval and posts a credit note.
